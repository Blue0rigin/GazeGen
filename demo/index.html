How to read this demonstration

Two reading purposes

Natural reading has no question available before reading. Information seeking gives the model the actual question as well as the text. For saved examples, the recorded readers had the corresponding experimental instruction.

Two kinds of trajectories

Generated paths are sampled predictions from one fixed GazeGen model. Human paths are individual observed records; a generated sample is not paired with a particular person's identity. Use “Next saved trajectory” to inspect variation.

Two versions of a passage

Advanced and Elementary express related content with different wording. Compare their coverage, revisits, and timing without assuming a simpler version always produces a shorter path. These examples do not measure subjective difficulty.

Data, display conventions, and limits

The model was trained only on OneStop first readings, with held-out readers and article families. The gallery retains the original three passages and adds nine pairs selected for closer generated–human agreement across both tasks and text versions. Each condition shows five of the 50 archived draws selected by mean normalized edit distance and duration-normalized ScaSim against all matched human readers. Every matching human record is retained. These curated examples illustrate successful predictions; they do not represent average performance or replace the full-test evaluation. The exact choices are recorded in the selection manifest. Events below 50 ms are omitted under the same display convention used for evaluation. Word positions are reflowed in this browser; lines do not reconstruct the original screen coordinates. Workload, comprehension, individual attention, and causal task effects cannot be inferred from an animation.

Source: OneStop · Berzak et al. (2025). This review package contains the model weights, inference code, and interactive prototype. The proposal describes the preliminary evaluation and results, and the technical supplement explains the model. Training and downstream experiment code are not included in this release.
